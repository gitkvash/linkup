2. Your phone's location

If you allow it, Linkup reads your phone's approximate location while the app is open, to centre the map and to suggest a place for a plan. We do not track you in the background and we do not store a history of where you have been. The position itself stays on your phone. What leaves it: the area of the map you are looking at (sent to our server and to the map provider to load plans and map tiles), and the place you deliberately choose for a plan, which is stored with the plan and shown to those who can see it. Refusing location permission only means you pick places by hand.

3. Who sees your data

  • Other Linkup users, as far as the app shows: your username, display name and bio are visible to other users and searchable; plans are visible according to their visibility setting; replies and declining reasons are visible to the plan's host, as shown in the app. Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Service providers who process data for us: our hosting and database providers; Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging for push, and Google Sign-In if you use it); our email provider, if you ask for a password-reset code; and Stadia Maps, which receives map tile requests (including the area you view and your IP address) and the text of place searches, with the map's centre point, to return results. These providers may process data outside Georgia and the European Economic Area under their own safeguards and terms.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising or share it with advertisers. The app does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. Legal bases

We process your data to perform our agreement with you (providing the service you signed up for), on the basis of your consent where you give it (location, push notifications, the record of your agreement; you can withdraw consent at any time in your phone settings or by deleting your account), and for our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and preventing abuse. Where applicable law is the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection or the EU General Data Protection Regulation, these are the grounds relied on.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data, plans, friendships, groups, notifications and push tokens: until you delete your account, which removes them. Plans you hosted are deleted with your account; your replies to other people's plans are removed with it. Notifications that other people have already received about you (for example "Nino is going to your plan") are kept in their own Alerts, because they belong to the recipient, until they clear them.
  • Reports are kept after either person deletes their account, for as long as needed to protect users and to deal with abuse or legal claims; they are visible only to us. Everything else about the people involved is deleted as above.
  • Sign-in sessions expire after 180 days of inactivity and end when you log out.
  • Password-reset codes: minutes, then discarded.
  • Server logs: kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.
  • Backups may keep deleted data for a short time before being overwritten.

6. Your rights

You can edit your name, bio and email in the app and delete your account and its data in Profile → Delete account. You also have the right to ask us for access to your data and a copy of it, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Write to {{CONTACT}} and we will answer without undue delay and within the time the law requires. You may also complain to the data protection authority: in Georgia, the Personal Data Protection Service; if you live in the EU, your local supervisory authority.

7. Security

Passwords are stored hashed. Traffic between the app and our server is encrypted. Access to data is restricted in the database so that an account can read only what it is allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your rights we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
